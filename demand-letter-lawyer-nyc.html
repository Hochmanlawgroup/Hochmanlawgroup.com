Why a letter from a lawyer changes the conversation

Your own emails and calls are easy to ignore. A demand letter on law firm letterhead is not. It tells the other side a licensed attorney has reviewed the claim and that a lawsuit is the next step. In many disputes, that is enough to produce payment or a serious settlement offer.
